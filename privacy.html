Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Trainboard ("we", "us") is a training dashboard and AI coaching app for endurance athletes. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Trainboard is currently in development and not yet open to the public.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and login details used to create a Trainboard account.
  • Garmin data: if you choose to connect your Garmin account, we receive the data you authorize through Garmin's official APIs. This may include activities (type, duration, distance, heart rate, power, pace, GPS), and daily health metrics such as sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, stress and training load.
  • Data you enter: training notes, goals, zones, plans and questions you ask the AI coach.

We never receive or store your Garmin password. Garmin sign-in happens on Garmin's own website.

How we use your data

  • To show you your own dashboard, trends, zones and training plan progress.
  • To generate AI coaching responses about your own training, only when you use the coach.
  • To operate, secure and fix the service.

Your data is only shown to you. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Trainboard. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud Platform: hosting and database storage (United States).
  • Anthropic (Claude API): AI processing of your training data to answer your coaching questions. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data is not used to train their models.
  • Authentication provider: account sign-in (account email only).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access tokens for connected services are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate Trainboard.

Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect Garmin at any time from within Trainboard or from your Garmin Connect account settings. When you disconnect, we stop receiving new Garmin data.
  • You can delete your account at any time. We delete your account and all associated data, including Garmin data, within 30 days.
  • If you revoke Trainboard's access from Garmin's side, we delete the Garmin data we hold for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. You can exercise these rights by contacting us.

Children

Trainboard is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and update the date at the top.
